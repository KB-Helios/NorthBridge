# NorthBridge brand

NorthBridge uses a single bridge glyph with a restrained Nordic-blue palette.
The public name is always written `NorthBridge`, with capital `N` and `B`.
The primary interface accent is approximately `#0857E0`; deep navy is reserved
for wordmark typography and high-contrast brand surfaces.

## Assets

- `AppIcon.appiconset/NorthBridge-AppIcon-1024.png` is the opaque 1024×1024
  production app icon. Do not add a rounded outer mask; iOS supplies it.
- `NorthBridgeMark.imageset/NorthBridgeMark.png` is the reusable square mark.
- `NorthBridgeWordmark.imageset/NorthBridgeWordmark.png` is the exact horizontal
  wordmark used by onboarding and sign-in.
- `scripts/Prepare-BrandAssets.ps1` creates opaque RGB derivatives and validates
  their dimensions.

The icon is deliberately simpler than the wordmark: one bridge form, one blue
range, and a pale neutral field. Avoid literal `NB` lettering, cable details,
flags, crowns, currency symbols, buildings, glass bubbles, and decorative
shadows.

## Final generation brief

> Create a production iPhone app icon for NorthBridge. Replace literal NB
> lettering with one exceptionally simple bridge arch fused with a subtle
> forward diagonal, so the negative space quietly suggests an N. Use precise
> rounded geometry, a porcelain-white to pale cool-gray field, and one rich
> Nordic blue with restrained tonal depth. Keep a compact centered silhouette
> that remains legible at small iPhone sizes. No text, cables, buildings,
> symbols, border, mockup, device frame, 3D extrusion, watermark, transparency,
> rounded canvas corners, or outer icon mask.
